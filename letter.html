Dear Ilsaa,

I know many people have told you this, and honestly, I’ve told you countless times myself: you are much stronger than you think you are.

But sometimes, even I forget that you’re human too. Even you need a place to cry, a shoulder to lean on, and someone to help carry the weight when things get too heavy. You come to us when you need support, but Ilsaa, you’ve handled so much on your own that it constantly reminds me of how incredibly strong you are.

(And no, this does NOT mean you can’t come to me! You definitely can. I’m always there for you, just like you’ve always been there for me.)

You’ve handled so much by yourself, and I know you’ll continue to face every challenge ahead with the same courage and strength.

And yes, your masti, your jokes, your drama, your tantrums, your sweet talks, your adorable gaaliyan, and most importantly, your vlog-style stories — you have no idea how much I love them and how much they entertain me. I genuinely can’t describe it. They’re so fun that I don’t even want to skip a single second of them.

So please, stay this charming girl forever. Grow up as much as you want. Become as mature as you want. But with me, stay this same childish, fun-loving girl for as long as possible… at least until I do something stupid!

But Ilsaa, truly, you are too awesome, too brilliant, too fabulous, too great, too fantastic, too exquisite, too stunning — and honestly, there aren’t enough adjectives in the dictionary to describe how amazing you are.

So keep smiling, don’t cry too much, and become as successful as you’ve always dreamed of being.

And yes, I’d happily be your patient any day. (Haan, thoda dar ka mahaul toh rahega, but it’s okay. Tere liye toh jaan bhi de denge. Yeh toh sirf ek-do daant ki baat hai!)

Baaki, tu toh Ilsaa hai… Ilsaa.

Yahan bhi hoga, wahan bhi hoga,

Kya tera hi jalwa!
